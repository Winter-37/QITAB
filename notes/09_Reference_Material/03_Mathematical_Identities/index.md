Identities used repeatedly in derivations, collected for look-up. Each one is stated without proof (the derivations live in Parts 0, 2 and 4) and checked numerically, so a sign or factor can be trusted when it is copied into a new derivation.

**Path.**

1. [[matrix-and-tensor-identities]] - trace and determinant rules, block inversion, Woodbury, Kronecker products and vec, Levi-Civita contractions.
2. [[commutator-and-anticommutator-identities]] - product rules, Jacobi, the Hadamard series, fermion anticommutators, commutators of excitation operators.
3. [[matrix-differentiation-identities]] - derivatives of inverses, determinants, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, the exponential and $\mathbf S^{\pm1/2}$; the toolkit of analytic gradients.
4. [[gaussian-integral-identities]] - Gaussian moments, the product theorem, the Boys function and the Coulomb energy of Gaussian charges.
5. [[pauli-matrix-identities]] - Pauli algebra and Pauli strings for spin and qubits.

**Needs.** Part 0, Chapter 1 (linear algebra) for the first three; second quantization (Part 4, Chapter 5) for the fermion identities.

**Leads to.** Analytic derivatives (Part 4, Chapter 15), molecular integrals (Part 4, Chapter 4) and qubit Hamiltonians (Part 6).

**Planned sections:** Vector-calculus identities; Spherical-tensor and 3j-symbol identities.
