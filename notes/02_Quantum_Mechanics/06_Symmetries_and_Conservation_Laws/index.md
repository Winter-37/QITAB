Symmetry operators (translation, parity, rotation), how they give conservation laws, degeneracy and selection rules, and the Wigner-Eckart theorem; then the Heisenberg picture and the path integral (Griffiths Ch. 6, with Sakurai companions).

**Path.** [[translation-operator-and-translational-symmetry]], [[parity-and-parity-selection-rules]] and [[rotation-operator-and-rotational-symmetry]] introduce the symmetry operators. Three Sakurai companions develop rotations further: [[rotations-su2-so3-and-euler-angles]], [[wigner-rotation-matrices]] and [[schwinger-oscillator-model]]. [[symmetry-conservation-and-degeneracy]] connects symmetry with conservation and degeneracy, and [[time-reversal-and-antiunitary-operators]] treats the one antiunitary symmetry in detail. [[rotational-selection-rules-and-wigner-eckart]] derives selection rules for scalars and vectors, and [[spherical-tensor-operators]] gives the general Wigner-Eckart theorem. [[heisenberg-picture-and-time-translation]] covers time translation, and [[feynman-path-integral]] reformulates time evolution as a sum over paths.
