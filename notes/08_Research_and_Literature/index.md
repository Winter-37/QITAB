Literature and research: one summary page per review or paper, each linked to the theory notes it draws on.

## What this part is about

Theory notes in Parts 4 and 6 explain methods in a uniform notation. The papers that introduced or applied those methods each have their own notation, assumptions and results. This part holds a summary page for each paper, written to the paper template, with the following parts:

- the question the paper asks;
- what it does;
- its key equations in the notebook's notation;
- its numbers;
- its limitations;
- links to the theory notes it relies on.

## Why it is here

- **Traceability.** Every claim in a theory note that comes from a paper can be checked against the summary and then against the paper itself.
- **Separation.** Keeping literature summaries apart from theory keeps theory notes from turning into reading lists.

## How to read it

Not in sequence: open a paper page when a theory note cites it.
