Summaries of review articles and review chapters: the overviews to read before the individual research papers of Chapter 3.

## What this chapter is about

A review collects a field's methods, notation and open problems in one place. Each summary here records what the review covers, which parts are most useful, and where its content is developed in the theory notes.

## Why it matters

- **VQE.** Tilly et al. cover every stage of the algorithm (Hamiltonians, encodings, measurement, ansätze, optimizers, error mitigation). Part 6, Chapter 3 draws on it.
- **DMET.** Wouters, Jiménez-Hoyos and Chan give the theory as of 2017: the bath construction, bath Hamiltonians and energy expressions. Part 4, Chapter 19 draws on it.
