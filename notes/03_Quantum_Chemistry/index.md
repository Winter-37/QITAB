Chemistry-level quantum chemistry: atoms, bonding, spectroscopy and reaction dynamics. It complements the method-oriented Part 4.

## What this part is about

Part 4 asks how to compute electronic energies. This part asks what those energies and wave functions mean for chemistry:

- atomic structure;
- molecular structure and bonding;
- qualitative molecular-orbital theory;
- spectroscopy (rotational, vibrational, electronic);
- reaction dynamics;
- potential energy surfaces as chemical objects.

## Why it is here

Methods are only useful when one knows what question to ask of them and how to interpret the answer. This part holds the chemical interpretation, so that Part 4 can stay focused on theory and algorithms.

## How to read it

Alongside Part 4, as context. Written so far, from Levine's *Quantum Chemistry*: Chapter 1 (atomic structure: lithium), Chapter 2 (molecular symmetry and point groups), Chapter 3 (chemical bonding), Chapter 4 (molecular-orbital theory), Chapter 6 (rotational and vibrational theory) and Chapter 9 (potential energy surfaces). The other chapters are planned. Chapters 3 and 4 can be read in either order; Chapter 4 supplies the canonical orbitals that Chapter 3 interprets.
