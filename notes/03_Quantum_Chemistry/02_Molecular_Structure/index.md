Molecular geometry and symmetry: symmetry elements and point groups, structural parameters, and how computed and measured structures are compared.

## What this chapter is about

The symmetry of the nuclear framework tells you, before any calculation, which orbitals can mix, which integrals vanish, whether the molecule can have a dipole moment, and whether it can be optically active. This chapter defines symmetry elements and operations, shows that they form a point group, and gives the classification of point groups with a procedure for assigning one.

## Why it matters

- **Free information.** Symmetry block-diagonalizes the Roothaan equations, labels orbitals and states, and gives selection rules.
- **Practical use.** Quantum chemistry programs detect the point group automatically to save work and to label results. Knowing what they detect, and when a slightly distorted geometry breaks the symmetry, avoids surprises.
