Planned chapter. Hartree–Fock, MCSCF, coupled-cluster amplitude equations, VQE parameter updates and geometry optimization are all minimizations or stationary-point searches. They share one toolkit: gradients and Hessians, Newton and quasi-Newton steps, trust regions that keep a step safe, and Lagrange multipliers for constraints and for non-variational energies. This chapter will state that toolkit once, so that the method chapters (Part 4, Chapters 7, 13 and 15; Part 6) can refer to it instead of re-deriving it.
