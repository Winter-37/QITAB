Planned chapter. Subspace methods measure Hamiltonian and overlap matrices in a small set of states prepared or generated on the device, then solve a generalized eigenvalue problem classically. Examples are quantum subspace expansion, Krylov and quantum-filter-diagonalization methods. They improve on a single VQE state and give excited states, but the measured overlap matrix is ill-conditioned and sensitive to noise.
