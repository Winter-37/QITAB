Planned chapter. Quantum phase estimation reads an energy eigenvalue directly from the phase $e^{-iEt}$ accumulated under time evolution. It needs only a state with good overlap with the target. It is the fault-tolerant route to full-CI-quality energies, with cost set by Hamiltonian simulation (Trotterization, qubitization) rather than by sampling noise. It is the long-term benchmark against which near-term methods are judged.
