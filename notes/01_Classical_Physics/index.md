The classical background for quantum mechanics and molecular simulation.

## What this part is about

Several ideas in quantum mechanics are taken over from classical physics and are easier to understand there first:

- the Hamiltonian and canonical variables;
- normal modes;
- electrostatic potentials and multipoles;
- partition functions;
- waves.

This part will state them in the form the later parts use.

## Why it is here

- **Hamiltonian mechanics** is the template for quantization (Part 2) and the equations of motion of ab initio molecular dynamics.
- **Normal modes** of small oscillations are the classical form of harmonic vibrational analysis (Part 4, Chapter 16).
- **Electrostatics and multipoles** underlie the Coulomb integrals, multipole methods and molecular properties of Part 4.
- **Statistical mechanics** turns energies into thermodynamic quantities such as enthalpies, free energies and rate constants.
