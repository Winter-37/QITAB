Planned chapter on the other mean-field route: density functionals within the Kohn–Sham scheme. The Hohenberg–Kohn theorems show that the ground-state density determines the energy. Kohn–Sham theory turns this into orbital equations with the same matrix structure as Hartree–Fock (Chapter 3). The exchange–correlation functional is unknown and must be approximated. Most applied quantum chemistry, including the routine geometry optimizations that wave-function methods are benchmarked against, uses DFT, so its strengths and failures are part of the context for every other chapter.
